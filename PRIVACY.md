# Sin & Sonnet Poster — Privacy Policy

Sin & Sonnet Poster is a private tool used only by Sin & Sonnet (sinandsonnet.com) to publish its own posts to its own Instagram account (@sinandsonnet) and its own Facebook Page.

- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data from other people.
- It only reads and writes posts on accounts owned by Sin & Sonnet.
- No data is used for advertising or analytics.
